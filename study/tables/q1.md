### Days until the limit and the blocks reach a size, demand permanently above capacity

Days from a steady chain at Z_M. `limit N MB`: the maximum block weight 2·M_N reaches N MB. `block N MB`: the first block of at least N MB is mined. `fill` = miners fill every block to the maximum regardless of fees (upper bound; blocks sit at the limit). `L0`..`L4` = rational miners, every user pays that wallet level; `auto` = wallet2 default priority; `auto_upm` = auto with users-pay-more.
